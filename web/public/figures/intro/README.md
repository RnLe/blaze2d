# Introduction figures and sources

- `periodicity.svg` is copied unchanged from the second-edition thesis repository,
  `master-thesis/assets/images/photonic_crystal_types.svg`. Chapter 2 introduces
  periodicity in one, two, and three directions.
- `electronic-photonic-crystals.png` is the comparison slide supplied by the author
  with the introduction-page request. The original is the 1565 × 801 clipboard
  image `codex-clipboard-d6cb61d8-0ad3-4bce-9395-c32dc9275895.png`.
- `band-structure.svg` is the existing introduction-page figure, retained unchanged.
  The thesis counterpart is
  `assets/figures/theoretical_background/monolayer_band_diagrams.svg`.
  Its generator, `assets/python/fig_monolayer_bands.py`, uses square rods
  (radius 0.2a, permittivity 8.9 in air) and triangular air holes
  (radius 0.48a, background permittivity 13). The thesis credits the canonical
  examples to Joannopoulos et al., Nature (1997). A gap along a symmetry path
  alone does not establish a complete gap.
- The five-by-two Bravais illustration is rendered as accessible SVG by
  `components/introduction-page/BravaisLattices.tsx`. Each bottom panel contains
  exactly 25 sites generated by integer combinations of the primitive vectors
  shown above it. The centered rectangular primitive vectors are (0.8, 0.5) and
  (-0.8, 0.5); the dashed conventional rectangle contains two primitive cells.
  Colors come from the site's design tokens.

Scientific text follows `sectionsV3/photonic_moire_crystals.typ`. Current Blaze
implementation claims were checked against `crates/core/src/operators/maxwell.rs`,
`analytic_geometry.rs`, `operator_data.rs`, and the website's configuration
reference. In particular, the current solver applies the full operators through
FFTs and extracts small projected matrices; it does not require a stored dense
plane-wave operator.

MPB's documented outputs and matrix-free method were checked against its official
[Python interface](https://mpb.readthedocs.io/en/latest/Python_User_Interface/) and
[developer documentation](https://mpb.readthedocs.io/en/latest/Developer_Information/).
The introduction distinguishes total runtime measurements from geometry-only
timings and makes no universal speedup claim.
